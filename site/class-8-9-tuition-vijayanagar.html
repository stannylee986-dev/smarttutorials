Class 8 & 9 Tuition in Vijayanagar, Bengaluru

For ICSE, CBSE and State students, at 9th Main Road, Vijayanagar II Stage

Maths gets more abstract in Class 8 and 9, and Science starts to split into Physics, Chemistry and Biology. A topic missed now often comes back in Class 10, when there is far less time to go over it again. Our Class 8 and 9 batches help students catch those gaps early, while there is still room to work on them calmly.

Is your child ready for this kind of support?

Tuition tends to help most when you notice one of these at home:

  • Marks have started slipping in one subject, even though the others are fine
  • Your child can follow the class but gets stuck on homework alone
  • Questions pile up because they feel shy to ask at school
  • Preparation starts only a day or two before each test

Questions parents ask

Should my child start tuition in Class 8, or wait for Class 10?

It depends on the child. If one subject is already difficult, starting earlier usually means fewer gaps to fill in Class 10. If school is going well, you may not need it yet. Call us or visit the centre, and we can talk it through.

Can my child join for only one subject?

Please call us on 097318 61427. We will tell you which subject batches are running and whether a one-to-one session would suit better.

Do you teach all three boards together?

We cover ICSE, CBSE and State syllabus. Tell us your child's board when you enquire, and we will explain how the batch works for it.
