About Us

A Tuition Centre for Students in Vijayanagar

Smart Tutorials, a unit of Smart Study Center, is on 9th Main Road, Vijayanagar II Stage, near Brooklyn National Public School. We teach Class 8, 9 and 10 students from ICSE, CBSE and State syllabus, in the Hampi Nagar and RPC Layout area of Vijayanagar. Students join us from across Vijayanagar, Bapuji Nagar, Chandra Layout, Nagarbhavi, Attiguppe and Kengeri.

We want every student to walk out of our classes sure of what they have learned, responsible for their own work and ready to do their very best.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions parents ask before joining.

Still have a question?

Talk to us directly. We're happy to help.

Call 097318 61427
What are the centre timings?

The centre is open Monday to Saturday, 10:00 AM to 9:00 PM, and closed on Sunday. Please call to confirm the batch timing for your class, as batches are planned around school hours.

Which classes and boards do you teach?

We teach Class 8, 9 and 10 students, and cover ICSE, CBSE and State syllabus.

Where is Smart Tutorials located?

822, 9th Main Road, near Brooklyn National Public School, Vijaya Nagar II Stage, Hampi Nagar, RPC Layout, Bengaluru – 560104.

Which areas do your students come from?

Students come to us from Vijayanagar, Bapuji Nagar, Chandra Layout, Nagarbhavi, Attiguppe, Kengeri, Hampi Nagar and RPC Layout. The centre is on 9th Main Road, Vijayanagar II Stage.

Do you offer one-to-one classes?

Yes. Along with regular batches, one-to-one sessions are available for students who need extra attention in a subject. Call us to check availability.

What are the fees?

Fees depend on the class, board and subjects. Please call 097318 61427 or visit the centre for the current fee details and refund terms, in writing, before you decide.

Do you guarantee marks or ranks?

No. We do not guarantee marks, ranks or admission anywhere. We focus on clear teaching, regular practice and doubt clearing. Results depend on each student.

How do I track my child's progress?

Students get regular tests, and parents can visit the office whenever needed to talk about how their child is doing.
