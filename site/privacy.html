Privacy Notice

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Who we are

Smart Tutorials, a unit of Smart Study Center, 822, 9th Main Road, Vijaya Nagar II Stage, Bengaluru 560104, decides how personal data shared with us for enquiries is used. This notice follows India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the rules made under it.

What this website collects

This website has no user accounts and does not save enquiry form details on any server. The form prepares a WhatsApp message on your own device. We receive the details only if you press send in WhatsApp.

What we receiveWhy
Parent or guardian name and mobile numberTo reply to your enquiry
Student's class and board, if you share themTo suggest the right batch
Anything you write in your messageTo answer your question

Please do not share sensitive details about a student's health or family in your message.

Children's data

Enquiries should come from a parent or guardian, or from a person aged 18 or older. We do not use this website to track children's behaviour or to show them targeted advertising. Students' faces in our gallery photos are blurred. We publish toppers' names, photos and results only with written consent from the student and parent.

Cookies and analytics

We use Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages are useful, but only if you click "Allow" on the cookie notice. Until then, no analytics code loads and no analytics cookies are set. If you allow it, Google Analytics sets cookies and receives your IP address, device and browser details and the pages you visit. We have turned off Google's advertising features and do not use this data to show anyone ads.

You can change your choice at any time with the "Cookie settings" link at the bottom of every page. Your choice itself is saved only in your own browser.

Other services

  • WhatsApp: messages you send are also handled under WhatsApp's own privacy terms.
  • Google Maps: the map loads only if you choose to load it. Google may then set cookies and receive your IP address. Links to Google Maps and Google reviews take you to Google's site.
  • Website hosting: our hosting provider may keep short-term technical logs, such as IP addresses, for security and to keep the site running.

Who sees your data and how long we keep it

Only staff who handle enquiries see your details. We do not sell them. We keep enquiry messages only as long as needed to respond and to run any admission that follows, unless the law requires us to keep them longer.

Your rights

You may ask us for a summary of your data, ask us to correct or erase it, withdraw your consent, raise a grievance, or nominate someone to act for you. Withdrawing consent is as easy as asking us; we will then stop using your details for this enquiry.

Contact and complaints

Call 097318 61427, email smartstudycenter2020@gmail.com, or write to us at the address above, marked "Privacy". If you are not satisfied with our reply, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.
